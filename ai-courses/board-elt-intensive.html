Why the intensive

Oversight fails at the handoff to management

A board can set the right expectations and still watch them dissolve in translation to the executive team. The intensive closes that gap by putting the board and the ELT in one room: the board learns to oversee, the executives see exactly what they'll be asked for, and together they build the artefacts and cadence that connect the two — so the instructions the board issues are actually operable.

It is the fastest route from "we should govern AI" to "here is our governance, owned and dated."
