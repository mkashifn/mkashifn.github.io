Important warning about the MSc route

An MSc should not be treated as a substitute for Irish medical registration.

MSc ≠ medical registration

A postgraduate degree normally does not automatically give a non-EU doctor permission to practise medicine.

MSc ≠ guaranteed medical job

You could finish the degree and still need to satisfy the Medical Council requirements.

MSc has opportunity cost

At age 43, one or two years outside clinical practice can be financially significant.

Strongest long-term family strategy if you obtain appropriate employment permission and can establish a stable residence.
