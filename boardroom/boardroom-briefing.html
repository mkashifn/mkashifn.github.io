The problem

AI adoption is outrunning AI oversight — and the board owns the gap

AI now sits inside decisions that used to be human — who gets credit, who gets shortlisted, what a customer is told. When those decisions go wrong, they go wrong at machine speed and scale. The director's duty of care does not switch off because an algorithm was involved, yet most boards cannot name every AI system operating inside their organisation.

The briefing closes that awareness gap fast, so the board can direct management with authority rather than delegate a risk it hasn't sized.
