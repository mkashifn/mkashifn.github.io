Why the cohort

Not every director can bring the whole board — this scales the competency

The masterclass is built for a single board. The certificate opens the same competency to individual directors — non-executive directors, committee chairs, and aspiring directors — who want the credential and the network without waiting for a whole-board engagement. The cross-company format is a feature: directors learn from how peers in other sectors are handling the same oversight problem.

A between-days assignment on each participant's own board makes day two grounded in reality, not theory.

Objective
Earn a recognised AI-governance credential and a working oversight toolkit — as an individual director.
Structure

Two half-days, with an assignment in between

Day 1 · half day
Foundations & the standard

Modules 1–4: why AI is a board issue, what ISO/IEC 42001 is, the regulatory convergence, and the four artefacts to demand.

Between days
Applied assignment

Each director inventories AI oversight on their own board against the four artefacts — bringing a real gap analysis to day two.

Day 2 · half day
Oversight, assurance & assessment

Modules 5–7: the oversight toolkit, the road to assurance, and the incident tabletop — then a short assessment and certification.
